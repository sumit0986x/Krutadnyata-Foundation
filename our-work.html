Vertical 01 • Healthcare Access

Health & Medical Camps: Bringing Preventive Care to Unreached Wadis

Healthcare access in remote Maharashtra talukas is severely hindered by distance and expense. We organize comprehensive free primary medical checkups, Ayurvedic consultations, and blood donation drives directly in villages and urban informal settlements.

Rural Ratnagiri & Solapur: Free Ayurvedic diagnostic camps conducted at Dhamapur Dhanawade Wadi (Sangameshwar), Pachal (Rajapur), and Sangola (Solapur) in association with Pushkar Ayurvedic Center.

COVID-19 Urban Screenings: On-ground primary health check-up camp at Bhatwadi, Ghatkopar amid the COVID-19 pandemic[cite: 1].

Voluntary Blood Drives: Regular camps organized at Manapurna Banquet (Borivali) with Sailee Degree College, CDS College of Engineering, and NSS units[cite: 1].

Vertical 02 • Equal Opportunity

Education & Career Guidance: Unlocking Competitive Futures

Financial limitations should never cap student ambition. We run competitive examination coaching, entrance test preparation for professional degrees, and career counseling for students navigating post-10th and post-12th crossroads[cite: 1].

Civil Services Coaching: Dedicated orientation programs with Aarya Institute of Administrative Services to increase youth representation in state and national administrative cadres[cite: 1].

Free MBA & Law Training: Zero-fee entrance test batches organized with AEC Trust, covering ₹20,000 course fees for 100 deserving students[cite: 1].

International University Scholarships: End-to-end admission guidance enabling students to secure funded placements across Germany, Ireland, the UK, and the USA[cite: 1].

Vertical 03 • Ecological Stewardship

Environment & Trees: Hands-on Ecological Preservation

Environmental responsibility is an active duty. We conduct physical conservation drives that remediate urban green belts, eliminate metal nail hazards from living trees, and plant native saplings in educational institutions[cite: 1].

Nail-Free Tree Campaign: Active de-nailing drive in Kandivali East removing heavy commercial iron nails from trees alongside 'Angholichi Goli'[cite: 1].

World Environment Day Drives: Large-scale tree plantation at Siddharth College (Badlapur, Thane) alongside educational trusts[cite: 1].

Green Business Symposia: Lectures for 300+ educators and students on adopting sustainable entrepreneurship by eminent environmentalist Dr. Rajashekhar Patil[cite: 1].

Vertical 04 • Athletics & Civic Pride

Youth, Sports & Fitness: Building Healthy Communities

Physical culture instills discipline, mental clarity, and camaraderie. We organize state-level marathons, self-defense workshops for girls, and supply sports equipment to ensure economic boundaries don't hold young athletes back[cite: 1].

State Sports Day Marathon 2026: Arranged in collaboration with the Directorate of Sports & Youth Services Maharashtra to commemorate Olympic wrestler KD Jadhav's legacy[cite: 1].

BIS Quality Run 2025: Over 1,200 participants mobilized in Borivali to champion standards, safety, and physical vitality[cite: 1].

Girls' Self-Defence Workshop: 250 junior college girls trained in martial art defense techniques alongside Rotary Club of Borivali[cite: 1].

School Uniform Distribution: Supplying proper athletic gear to underprivileged students at Harchand Lokhande High School[cite: 1].

Vertical 05 • Direct Humanitarian Aid

Community Relief: Direct Lifelines in Times of Need

When unexpected medical crises strike marginalized families or harsh winter weather hits homeless populations, we deploy direct emergency grants and essential supplies without administrative delays[cite: 1].

₹3,00,000 Emergency Medical Aid: Financial donation disbursed directly to the family of an ailing patient from Pune for critical medical treatment[cite: 1].

Winter Blanket Drives: Free blanket distributions to poor and homeless citizens in Borivali West and Gorai[cite: 1].

Rajgruha Mahaparinirvan Diwas Relief: Distribution of packaged drinking water and biscuits to thousands paying homage to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar in Matunga[cite: 1].

Public Safety Sessions: Cyber security and social media scam prevention seminars for students conducted with certified ethical hackers[cite: 1].

Vertical 06 • Livelihood & Justice

Skill Development & Free Legal Aid: Sustainable Self-Reliance

Charity solves immediate distress, but vocational skills and legal protection ensure long-term autonomy. We run government-certified IT and commerce vocational centers, paired with ongoing free legal clinics for citizens[cite: 1].

Aarya Skill Development Centre: Running certified courses in Accounts Executive and Web Development with the Maharashtra State Skill Development Corporation (MSSDC)[cite: 1].

Free Legal Advice Centre: Bi-monthly consultation clinics conducted on 2nd & 4th Saturdays in Mumbai in association with Wonders Group[cite: 1].

Standards Clubs in Schools: Partnered with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) to establish Standards Clubs fostering consumer safety culture in youth[cite: 1].
