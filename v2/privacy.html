Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Krutadnyata Foundation (कृतज्ञता फाउंडेशन) is committed to safeguarding personal information collected through our online portals, volunteer desks, donor records, and on-ground health and legal clinics across Maharashtra.

Introduction & Legal Entity

This Privacy Policy outlines how Krutadnyata Foundation (referred to as “the Foundation”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), a registered public social organisation established under the laws of Maharashtra, collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data obtained through our official website and offline community welfare interventions.

By accessing our web portal, submitting volunteer applications, enrolling in vocational skilling batches, registering for marathons, or making voluntary charitable contributions, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

Categories of Data We Collect

We only gather personal information that is reasonably necessary to fulfill our statutory, charitable, and administrative responsibilities:

  • Donor Information: Full legal name, contact address, email address, phone number, and Permanent Account Number (PAN) where legally required under the Income Tax Act, 1961 for issuance of Form 10BE and 80G tax exemption receipts.
  • Volunteer & Event Participants: Full name, WhatsApp contact number, academic institution or workplace, city of residence, athletic emergency contacts (for marathon participants), and participation hours.
  • Institutional & CSR Partners: Corporate representative names, corporate identity numbers (CIN), business correspondence addresses, and formal memorandum details.
  • Beneficiary & Clinic Records: Patient demographics, contact phone numbers, and case summaries collected strictly for attendance, doctor consult allocation, or medical grant dispersal.

How Your Information is Utilized

All personal information entrusted to Krutadnyata Foundation is used strictly for legitimate charitable purposes, including:

  • Generating and issuing authenticated digital donation receipts and statutory 80G certificates.
  • Coordinating volunteer rosters and issuing verified participation certificates for NSS and academic hours.
  • Communicating schedule notices for upcoming rural health screenings, blood donation camps, or free legal advice sessions.
  • Maintaining compliance with the Charity Commissioner, Maharashtra State, the Income Tax Department, and statutory regulatory filings.
  • Transmitting quarterly newsletters and programmatic activity reports (subject to recipient opt-out at any time).

Beneficiary, Patient & Clinic Confidentiality

A core pillar of our social responsibility is protecting the dignity and personal security of the vulnerable groups we serve:

  • Free Legal Advice Clinic: Case files, consultation notes, matrimonial inquiries, and family disputes handled during our bi-monthly legal clinics with Wonders Group are strictly confidential and protected under client privilege protocols. Under no circumstances are legal case notes published or shared.
  • Emergency Medical Grants: While financial disbursals (such as the ₹3,00,000 medical grant to Prakash Gangurde) are recorded for public audits, individual medical history, diagnostic charts, and personal home details are kept secure and shared only with certified medical panels or statutory auditors.
  • Marginalized Group Protection: Specific protection protocols are maintained for records concerning the transgender community, women in distress, and Divyangjan beneficiaries, preventing any stigmatizing exposure.

80G Tax Compliance & Payment Processing

Online donations to Krutadnyata Foundation are processed through certified, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (including Razorpay).

Credit/Debit Card & NetBanking Details: The Foundation does not store, process, or have access to your full credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking passwords. All payment transactions are encrypted via 256-bit Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption through the gateway.

PAN & Tax Filings: Under Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) notifications, donor PAN numbers are mandatory to report philanthropic contributions on the Income Tax Department portal for Form 10BE generation. This data is handled exclusively by authorized accounting personnel.

Cookies & Digital Analytics

Our website uses basic, privacy-friendly technical cookies to ensure smooth website navigation, maintain session state, and evaluate general website traffic metrics (e.g., page load speeds, popular project pages).

We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers, behavioral profiling scripts, or data-broker trackers. You can modify your browser settings to disable cookies, although certain layout features may adjust accordingly.

Data Storage, Retention & Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Digital records are stored on secure cloud servers utilizing encrypted databases and role-based access controls.
  • Physical attendance sheets from field camps are archived under lock and key at the foundation's administrative desk.
  • Donation transaction logs are preserved for a minimum period of 7 years in compliance with the Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950 and the Income Tax Act.

Your Data Protection Rights

Under prevailing Indian data protection laws, visitors, donors, and volunteers enjoy the following rights regarding their personal records:

  • Right to Review: You may request a summary of personal information held about you in our records.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete donor or contact records.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe from routine newsletters, email updates, or volunteer message groups at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link or contacting our secretariat.
