Where It Began

Healthcare that travels to where it's needed most.

Our first camps were small — a handful of volunteers and a borrowed hall in Gorai. Today, free medical and Ayurvedic check-ups reach rural talukas like Sangola and Sangameshwar, where the nearest clinic can be hours away.

Growing Roots

Protecting the ecosystems our communities depend on.

The Nail-Free Tree Campaign and our Badlapur sapling drives grew out of a simple observation: a thriving community needs a thriving environment. We now run ongoing green initiatives alongside our welfare programs.

Opening Doors

Skills and guidance for the next generation.

Through the Aarya Skill Centre and partnerships with MSSDC, we offer government-certified vocational training, competitive exam coaching, and career mentorship to youth who would otherwise have no access to either.

Milestones

Our Journey So Far

2019

Foundation Established

Krutadnyata Foundation begins operations in Suburban Mumbai with our first health camps and blood donation drives.

2021

Environmental Drives Launch

The Nail-Free Tree Campaign and World Environment Day sapling plantations begin in Badlapur and Kandivali.

2023

Aarya Skill Centre Opens

Government-certified vocational batches begin under MSSDC affiliation, training youth in web development and accounts.

2026

1,200+ Runner Marathon

The State Sports Day Marathon becomes our largest civic mobilization yet, run with the Directorate of Sports Maharashtra.
