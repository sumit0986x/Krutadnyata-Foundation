Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver dignity-first healthcare, education, and environmental action directly to Maharashtra's most overlooked communities — through free medical camps, vocational and competitive-exam training, tree-protection drives, and timely relief — while building the local partnerships that keep this work sustainable.

Our Vision

A Maharashtra where no family forgoes care for distance, no student forgoes opportunity for circumstance, and every community has the skills, green cover, and civic trust to look after itself and its neighbors — gratitude, sustained as everyday action.

Our Story

How We Grew

Three chapters, one thread — a small team of volunteers answering whatever gap stood in front of them next.

01
Where It Began

Healthcare that travels to where it's needed most.

Our first camps were small — a handful of volunteers and a borrowed hall in Gorai. Today, free medical and Ayurvedic check-ups reach rural talukas like Sangola and Sangameshwar.

Turning Point
02
Growing Roots

Protecting the ecosystems our communities depend on.

The Nail-Free Tree Campaign and our Badlapur sapling drives grew from one idea: a thriving community needs a thriving environment too.

03
Opening Doors

Skills and guidance for the next generation.

Through the Aarya Skill Centre and partnerships with MSSDC, we offer government-certified vocational training and career mentorship to youth who'd otherwise have no access to either.

Milestones

Our Journey So Far

2019

Foundation Established

Founded the Free Legal Advice Centre, ran the first Nail-Free Tree campaign, and held a self-defence workshop for 250 junior college girls.

2020

Health Outreach & COVID Relief

Ayurvedic camps in Sangola and Sangameshwar, plus frontline health screenings at Bhatwadi and Ghatkopar during COVID-19.

2021

Environmental Drives Launch

The Nail-Free Tree Campaign and World Environment Day sapling plantations begin in Badlapur and Kandivali.

2022

Civil Services Training Drive

Dedicated IAS/UPSC awareness and training programs with Aarya Academy for Maharashtra youth.

2023

Aarya Skill Centre Opens

Government-certified vocational batches begin under MSSDC affiliation, training youth in web development and accounts.

2024

Medical Grant Aid & Free Coaching

₹3,00,000 donated for critical medical treatment in Pune, plus free blanket distribution and MBA/Law entrance training.

2025

BIS Clubs & Quality Run

Formed BIS Standard Clubs in colleges and organized the Quality Run in Borivali with 1,200+ citizens.

2026 Present

1,200+ Runner Marathon

The State Sports Day Marathon becomes our largest civic mobilization yet, run with the Directorate of Sports Maharashtra.
