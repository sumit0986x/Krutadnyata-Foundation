Strategic Scope

Justice without the price tag

For many of Mumbai's underserved residents, a lawyer's consultation fee alone is enough to put justice out of reach. In association with Wonders Group, Krutadnyata Foundation runs a dependable, recurring Free Legal Advice Center that offers plain-language legal guidance — without an appointment fee and without eligibility gatekeeping — to anyone who walks in.

Consultation Clinic
Bi-Monthly Consultation Clinics

A fixed, predictable schedule residents can plan around

Free legal advice sessions are conducted on the 2nd and 4th Saturdays of every month in Mumbai, in association with Wonders Group. The fixed, recurring schedule means residents know exactly when help is available, removing the uncertainty that often keeps people from seeking legal guidance in the first place.

Priority Access for Women & Families

Dedicated guidance on the disputes that affect daily life most

The clinic gives dedicated attention to women, families, and marginalized groups navigating matrimonial, property, and labour disputes — the categories of dispute most likely to be ignored or mishandled when legal counsel is unaffordable. Advisors walk each visitor through their options in plain language before any formal step is taken.

Women & Families First

"Justice shouldn't come with a price tag. If you can walk in on a Saturday and walk out understanding your rights, we've done our job."

Krutadnyata Foundation, Field Note — Mumbai
Chronological Timeline

An ongoing service, not a one-off

Clinic established in the Foundation's earliest years Since 2019 · Mumbai

Free legal consultation began running continuously since Krutadnyata Foundation's founding years, with no appointment fee and no eligibility gatekeeping from day one.

Partnership with Wonders Group formalized Ongoing · Mumbai

Wonders Group joined as the Foundation's legal partner, facilitating pro-bono consultation clinics for Mumbai's underserved communities and helping standardize the bi-monthly schedule.

Fixed bi-monthly clinic schedule adopted Recurring · 2nd & 4th Saturday of every month

The clinic settled into its current, predictable rhythm — the 2nd and 4th Saturdays of every month — so residents always know when free legal advice is available.

Priority service for women, families & marginalized groups Ongoing · Walk-in basis

The clinic continues today as a dependable, recurring service, with dedicated guidance for women, families, and marginalized groups navigating matrimonial, property, and labour disputes.
