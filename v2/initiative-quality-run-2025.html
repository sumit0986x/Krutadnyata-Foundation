BIS Standard Clubs & Borivali Quality Run

A joint initiative with the Bureau of Indian Standards to build a culture of quality, consumer safety consciousness, and physical health among youth — through collegiate clubs and a high-participation civic marathon that brought 1,200+ citizens onto the streets of Borivali.

Strategic Scope

Cultivating a quality-consciousness culture from an early age

In a modern consumer society, awareness of product safety, standardized manufacturing, and statutory testing is as critical as academic knowledge. Under our formal agreement with the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), Krutadnyata Foundation initiated dedicated Standards Clubs in schools and colleges across Borivali — the first of their kind run by a Maharashtra NGO in direct partnership with BIS Mumbai Branch-I.

Standards Club
Educational Competitions

Quizzes, debates, and product-verification workshops

Standards Clubs are designed to educate students from young ages about standardized quality benchmarks through interactive classroom quizzes, essay competitions, debates, and industrial safety presentations held in partner institutions. The overarching mission is to develop quality-conscious consumers and future leaders who prioritize consumer safety and excellence across industry and daily life.

Quality Run Marathon

Mobilizing a city block by block, one run at a time

On 25 January 2025, BIS Mumbai Branch Office-I, in association with Sailee Degree College and Krutadnyata Foundation, hosted the flagship Quality Run — uniting over 1,200 participants committed to spreading standards consciousness through mass civic athletics. The run carried the club's classroom message of consumer safety out of the campus and onto the streets of Borivali.

Chronological Timeline

Program roadmap & on-ground milestones

Formation of Standard Clubs in schools & colleges 25 January 2025 · Borivali, Mumbai

Krutadnyata Foundation, in association with the Bureau of Indian Standards, opened Standard Clubs across participating institutions to instill quality consciousness.

The Quality Run — over 1,200 participants 25 January 2025 · Sailee Degree College

BIS Mumbai Branch Office-I, Sailee Degree College, and Krutadnyata Foundation jointly hosted the flagship run uniting over 1,200 participants committed to excellence.

Press Information Bureau national release Late January 2025 · National media release

An official PIB press release acknowledged the multi-tier collaboration between BIS, academic institutions, and Krutadnyata Foundation.

Momentum carries into State Sports Day Ongoing · With the Directorate of Sports & Youth Services Maharashtra

The Quality Run's organizing playbook now feeds directly into the Foundation's wider sports vertical, including the State Sports Day Marathon run with the Directorate of Sports & Youth Services Maharashtra.
