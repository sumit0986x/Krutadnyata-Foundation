Strategic Scope

Unlocking competitive futures regardless of family income

A meritorious student should never be priced out of a civil services career, a professional degree, or a foreign university seat. Krutadnyata Foundation runs a three-tier academic empowerment vertical — competitive examination coaching, zero-fee entrance test preparation for professional degrees, and end-to-end career counseling — so that students navigating the post-10th, post-12th, and post-graduate crossroads can compete on talent, not on their ability to pay coaching fees.

Civil Services Coaching
UPSC / MPSC Orientation

Dedicated orientation with the Aarya Institute of Administrative Services

Working with the Aarya Institute of Administrative Services, we run structured orientation programs for civil services aspirants — covering the UPSC and MPSC examination pattern, combined PSI/STI/ASO preparation, and police recruitment guidance. The goal is to increase first-generation and economically disadvantaged youth representation in state and national administrative cadres, by closing the information and mentorship gap that otherwise keeps talented aspirants from even attempting the exam.

Free MBA & Law Entrance Training

Zero-fee batches with AEC Trust — ₹20,000 covered for 100 students

In partnership with AEC Trust, we organize zero-fee entrance test preparation batches for MBA and Law aspirants, with the Foundation covering ₹20,000 in course fees for 100 deserving students who could not otherwise afford structured entrance coaching. Sessions cover CET-pattern reasoning, verbal ability, and legal aptitude — the same syllabus commercial coaching centres charge thousands of rupees to teach.

Chronological Timeline

Program roadmap & on-ground milestones

Civil services orientation launched with Aarya Institute Academic Empowerment Vertical · Launch Phase

Krutadnyata Foundation and the Aarya Institute of Administrative Services began joint UPSC/MPSC orientation sessions, including combined PSI/STI/ASO and police recruitment guidance, for first-time aspirants.

AEC Trust partnership funds 100 free MBA & Law seats ₹20,000 entrance fees covered per student

A formal arrangement with AEC Trust opened zero-fee entrance test preparation batches for MBA and Law aspirants, with the Foundation underwriting course fees for 100 deserving students.

International scholarship advisory desk opens Germany · Ireland · UK · USA

End-to-end admission counseling began helping students shortlist universities, prepare applications, and secure funded placements across Germany, Ireland, the UK, and the USA.

750+ aspirants mentored and counting Ongoing · Across all three program tiers

Combined enrolment across civil services orientation, free entrance batches, and scholarship advisory has now crossed 750 aspirants mentored, with 100% free access to entrance test preparation.
