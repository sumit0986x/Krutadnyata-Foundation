Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding voluntary donations, participation, and partnership MOUs.

How can institutions or colleges partner with Krutadnyata Foundation?

We frequently partner with degree and junior colleges for NSS blood donation camps[cite: 1], BIS Standard Clubs[cite: 1], cyber-security awareness sessions[cite: 1], and government-accredited skill training[cite: 1]. You can submit a partnership request above or write to our secretariat.

How does one book an appointment for the Free Legal Advice Centre?

Free legal advice sessions take place on the 2nd and 4th Saturday of every month between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM[cite: 1]. To ensure adequate time with the legal advisors, visits are scheduled by prior appointment only via WhatsApp at +91 98338 61159[cite: 1].

Are donations eligible for tax exemptions?

Krutadnyata Foundation is a registered public charitable trust. Donors seeking receipts and 80G tax exemption verification can review our trust credentials or contact our accounts desk directly [TO CONFIRM: 80G Approval Status].

Do student volunteers receive official certificates?

Yes. Active participants supporting our annual marathons[cite: 1], tree plantations[cite: 1], health camps[cite: 1], and civic drives receive formal participation and social service certificates recognizing their on-ground hours.
