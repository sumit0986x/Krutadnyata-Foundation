Strategic Scope

Converting vocational training into real, certified employability

Charity meets an immediate need, but sustainable self-reliance comes from livelihood skills. The Aarya Skill Development Centre is Krutadnyata Foundation's flagship skilling vertical — a government-affiliated training institute delivering certified vocational batches so that local youth leave not just with knowledge, but with a recognised, employable credential.

Web Development
MSSDC-Affiliated Course

A government-certified Web Development batch, built for local youth

Run in affiliation with the Maharashtra State Skill Development Corporation, the Web Development batch takes trainees through practical, job-ready coursework — from core fundamentals to building real projects — culminating in a government-recognised certification that strengthens their standing in the job market.

Accounts Executive Batch

Commerce skills that open the door to entry-level finance roles

Alongside Web Development, the Centre runs a certified Accounts Executive batch covering bookkeeping, taxation basics, and office finance workflows — preparing trainees for entry-level roles in accounts and back-office functions across small and mid-sized businesses in the region.

Chronological Timeline

Program roadmap & on-ground milestones

Centre established & MSSDC affiliation secured Formation phase · Suburban Mumbai

Krutadnyata Foundation set up the Aarya Skill Development Centre and secured formal affiliation with the Maharashtra State Skill Development Corporation, enabling government-recognised certification for trainees.

First certified Accounts Executive & Web Development batches Early intake cohort

The Centre enrolled its first cohorts in the two flagship courses, combining classroom instruction with hands-on practical sessions ahead of government certification exams.

Standards Clubs launched with BIS In partnership with the Bureau of Indian Standards

Alongside the vocational batches, Krutadnyata Foundation partnered with the Bureau of Indian Standards to establish Standards Clubs in schools, fostering a consumer safety culture among students from a young age.

Ongoing intake & expansion Ongoing · New batches enrolling

The Centre continues to run successive MSSDC-certified batches, with the Foundation working toward expanding course offerings and intake capacity to reach more local youth.
