Our Mission

Krutadnyata Foundation exists to give back to society with genuine gratitude. We organize free medical camps, career orientation and state-accredited vocational training for youth, environmental protection drives, voluntary blood donation camps, and direct emergency relief for families in need — collaborating hand in hand with colleges, statutory bodies, and grassroots communities.

Bridging The Divides

Reaching remote rural wadis and congested urban wards with equal resolve.

Marginalized populations often fall through the gaps of municipal programs. In remote talukas like Sangola (Solapur) and Pachal (Rajapur), basic health screenings can save lives by diagnosing conditions before they become catastrophic emergencies.

Meanwhile, in urban belts like Gorai, Borivali, and Ghatkopar, young students frequently lack the mentorship needed to navigate competitive exams or global admissions. Through our partnerships with academic institutions, we provide free UPSC/MPSC guidance, free entrance training for MBA and Law, and government-certified vocational courses.

Holistic Inclusion

Direct aid, transgender community support, women's self-reliance, and Divyangjan assistance.

Verifiable Track Record

Our Journey Since 2019

Every year marked by focused grassroots delivery.

2026 Present Work

State Sports Day Marathon

Organized the State Sports Day Marathon on 24th January 2026 in collaboration with the Directorate of Sports & Youth Services, Maharashtra State, and District Sports Council, facilitated by Sailee Degree College and Jaywant Harchand Lokhande Law College to honor Olympic medalist Khashaba Dadasaheb Jadhav.

2025

BIS Standards Club, Quality Run & Civic Safety

Formed Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Standard Clubs in colleges. Organized the Quality Run in Borivali with 1,200+ citizens (covered by PIB). Conducted school uniform donations at Harchand Lokhande High School, Cyber Security awareness for classes 5–11, and Food Act adulteration sessions with retired FDA officials.

2024

Medical Grant Aid & Free Entrance Coaching

Donated ₹3,00,000 for critical medical treatments to an ailing patient from Pune. Conducted free blanket distribution in Gorai, sponsored Chem Cruise, held college blood donation drives with NSS Sailee, and arranged free MBA/Law entrance test training with AEC Trust.

2023

State Skill Development Centre (MSSDC)

Inaugurated Aarya Skill Development Centre in association with Maharashtra State Skill Development Corporation to offer market-relevant vocational training in Accounts Executive and Web Development for youth employability. Celebrated 11th International Yoga Day.

2022

Civil Services Training Drive

Conducted dedicated awareness and training programs for Indian Administrative Services (IAS/UPSC) with Aarya Academy to increase representation of Maharashtra youth in state and national civil services.

2021

World Environment Day & Digital Guidance

Planted trees in Badlapur, hosted a 300-participant "Green Business" environmental seminar with Dr. Rajashekhar Patil, conducted a tri-lingual environment letter-writing contest, and organized competitive exam orientation for 100 students online.

2020

Regional Health Outreach & COVID Relief

Conducted Ayurvedic health camps in Sangola (Solapur) with Pushkar Center, medical camp at Pachal (Rajapur) under Vaidya Mangesh Khade, rural clinic at Sangameshwar (Ratnagiri), and essential frontline health screenings at Bhatwadi, Ghatkopar during COVID-19.

2019

Establishment & Founding Interventions

Founded the Free Legal Advice Centre (operating 2nd and 4th Saturdays in Mumbai). Executed the Nail-Free Tree campaign in Kandivali East, self-defense workshop for 250 junior college girls with Rotary Club of Borivali, "Sadbhavna Samvad" lecture for 350 students, and Matunga homage food assistance.
