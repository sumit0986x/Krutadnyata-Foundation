Questions About Giving

Can I specify which initiative my funds support?

Yes. You can select your preferred vertical on our form—whether rural medical camps[cite: 1], educational exam coaching[cite: 1], or emergency community relief[cite: 1]. We disburse restricted funds exclusively to the chosen program.

How soon will I receive my tax receipt?

For online gateway donations, receipts are emailed instantly to the address entered at checkout. For direct NEFT/RTGS bank transfers, our accounts desk generates your stamped 80G certificate within 2 business days of transaction confirmation.

Do you accept corporate CSR partnerships?

Yes. We provide end-to-end CSR project implementation across Maharashtra, complete with baseline surveys, photographic documentation, beneficiary lists, and chartered accountant-audited utilization certificates[cite: 1].
