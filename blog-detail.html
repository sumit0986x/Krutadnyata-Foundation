State Sports Day Marathon 2026: Inspiring Community Wellness Across Suburban Mumbai

How Krutadnyata Foundation collaborated with the Directorate of Sports and Youth Services, Maharashtra State, Sailee Degree College, and Lokhande Law College to mark Khashaba Jadhav's legacy.

कृ

Foundation Secretariat & Field Desk

Verified On-Ground Report • Page 2 of Work Report
Marathon 2026 Starting Line & Crowd Gathering Suburban Mumbai • 24 January 2026 • Work Report Pg 2
Photo: Directorate & KF Field Archives

On the morning of January 24, 2026, hundreds of students, athletes, and local citizens assembled in Suburban Mumbai to take part in the State Sports Day Marathon 2026. Arranged as part of Krutadnyata Foundation's continuing commitment to health, athletic discipline, and community cohesion, the marathon celebrated an enduring legacy in Maharashtra's rich sporting history.

“Sports and athletic discipline are vital equalizers. When young students run together, barriers of economic status, community division, and background dissolve.”

Honoring Olympic Medalist Khashaba Dadasaheb Jadhav

Maharashtra celebrates its State Sports Day annually in honor of the birth anniversary of Khashaba Dadasaheb Jadhav, who made history at the 1952 Helsinki Summer Olympics by securing independent India's first individual Olympic medal in freestyle wrestling.

To honor this historic feat, Krutadnyata Foundation envisioned the 2026 Marathon not merely as a competitive race, but as a broad civic movement to encourage healthy living, reduce youth lifestyle stress, and introduce young students from underprivileged backgrounds to institutional athletics.

State Institutional Collaboration

Grassroots impact achieves genuine scale when civic organizations collaborate with statutory state bodies. The 2026 Marathon was executed in official collaboration with:

  • Directorate of Sports and Youth Services, Maharashtra State
  • District Sports Council & District Sports Officer, Suburban Mumbai
  • Sailee Degree College (Facilitation partner & NSS student volunteer deployment)
  • Jaywant Harchand Lokhande Law College (Legal coordination & venue facilitation)
Key Milestone

Over 1,200 Citizen Participants

Building upon the success of the 2025 BIS Quality Run, this year's marathon mobilized families, junior college athletes, and senior citizens across Suburban Mumbai along safe, municipal-sanctioned running corridors.

Volunteer Marshalling & On-Ground Safety

Managing a high-density urban run requires meticulous logistical planning. Over 40 trained Krutadnyata Foundation volunteers, working hand in hand with local traffic personnel, manned hydration desks, first-aid checkpoints, and participant registration lanes starting at 5:00 AM.

Free participation kits, hydration fluids, and completion certificates were provided to every runner. Special recognition and sports kit donations were presented to promising junior athletes from municipal high schools to support their training journey.

What Lies Ahead for Youth Sports in 2026

The marathon is part of a larger ongoing athletic initiative. Following the distribution of athletic uniforms at Harchand Lokhande High School in 2025, Krutadnyata Foundation is expanding its local coaching clinics, inter-school athletics sponsorships, and sports scholarship facilitation for youth throughout the calendar year.
